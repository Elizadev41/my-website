
# Baking Recipe Finder

A simple recipe search app for finding baking recipes quickly.

**Live Demo:** [https://elizadev41.github.io/my-website/](https://elizadev41.github.io/my-website/)

## What's This?

A straightforward recipe finder focused on baking. Search for what you want to bake and get the full recipe with ingredients and steps.

## What You Can Do

* **Search Recipes** – Type what you want to bake (cookies, brownies, cake, etc.)
* **View Multiple Results** – If multiple recipes match, browse them all
* **See Full Details** – Get ingredients, steps, time, servings, and difficulty
* **Access Original Sources** – Links to the original recipes from AllRecipes

## How to Use

1. Type what you want to bake in the search box
2. Click "Find Recipes" or press Enter
3. Browse results if multiple recipes match
4. Click "View Recipe" to see the full instructions
5. Follow the ingredients and steps to start baking

## Why I Made This

I wanted a simple way to search through my favorite baking recipes without opening multiple tabs or scrolling through long blog posts. This keeps everything in one place and makes it easy to find what I need.
